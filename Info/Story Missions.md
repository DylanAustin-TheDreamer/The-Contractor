# Prologue

### Intro
We first enter the game and the player is told that they have received an encrypted signal from another galaxy. The individuals in question, work for a contracting company looking for users to test their simulation for hybrid robotics.

You enter in and take control of a cyborg named 'D6' whom you really know nothing about. The first scene begins in a large futuristic city, and D6 walks out of a 'Cellular Wi-Fi' box after receiving the JSON response.

# Chapter 1 - The Fall and Rise... of D6

### Intro

When D6 remembered his family, was shot in the head and thrown into space, a scrapper passing a particular solar trade route stops when realising the figure floating in space was a cyborg - a very very expensive one.

It is known amongst certain communities, that there were once upon a time, specific models of hybrid robotics that went out of commission for various reasons, but they were extremely effective when functional. To find one of these cyborgs was like finding a gold mine, and the scrapper who found D6 could not resist his temptation.

However, when the scrapper brought D6 back to his workshop in his home solar system, he scanned D6 and realised his organs were actually still intact and functional. The bullet that had penetrated D6's head missed the 27% left of his brain, by a fractionate amount. So, the scrapper brought D6's electronics and life support back online.

After having done so, and earning his keep in order to gain a ship of his own... D6 plans to stamp out the entire contracting company once and for all... It all starts with an ambitious hitlist, and what he is trained to do by trade... tracking them all down one by one.
