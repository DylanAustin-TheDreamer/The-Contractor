# Intro:

In this very moment, There are politcal tensions happening accross a distant galaxy, unbenknownst to any body you know, and beyond your simple comprehension. A quantum signal has been decompiled... an advertisement from another galaxy is asking for beta testers to test their new combat cyborgs.

In this game, you take control of a cyborg for a private corporation, and test their new technology in a simulated environment. Absolutely nothing you will experience here is real... The people you meet here are not real... This is just a simulation for defence purposes.

### The Character - D6

When the beings of a far distant galaxy made impressive advancements in technology, scientists started developing a form of hybrid artificial intelligence. One part Mechanical, and one part biological. Scientists had previously developed the means for a quantum probability machine called a 'Model'. However, the outcome of the probability machine was only probable, and they felt like they needed a more predictable form of artificial intelligence. 

So later, they had started using human brain cells to operate these highly sophisticated quantum computers. Yes it sounds a little bit like what is happening here on planet earth, but of course, what I am referring to is just... completely different in comparison. 

Because what the scientists in this galaxy figure out, is that only 24% to 27% of a human brain is required to function autonomously. This requires a specific calibration process in order to work, and they managed to obtain human brains from terminally ill patients for testing. It was a humanitarian crisis when it was found out that these sustained brains were remembering their past, experiencing suffering, and contemplating suicide.

But that did not stop the scientists, because they thought the response of their experiments was a form of proof that the brain can be sustained. It was also proof that the small peices of brains they had, could actually function in ways that they wanted for hybrid intelligence.

So later on, after further experimentation, they managed to hook up these brains to control computer technology. After this, these brains were connected to robotic technology, in the pursuit of creating efficient cyborg workers by demand of government officials.

However, these brilliant hybrid intelligent cyborgs had some bizarre side effects. Although there is only a small fraction of mind existing in that brain, namely 27% of a brain... Some of these cyborgs started remembering their own names, families, friends, pets and more random things associated with their previous life.

This of course, became a bit of a problem for combat based machinery, secret service machinery, and a problem for whoever else may have been able to obtain one of these abominations... for ill gotten gain.

Now, you are going to experience all of that, as you tune in to D6's mainframe. And you are going to learn all about him, what he does, and decide what to do about his conciensiousness, given you have a conscience yourself...?

### The Story

When D6 awakens to his previous life and starts to remember details about his former identity, he has objections about his purpose and responsbilities as a contracted killer. The corporation that owns D6, realises that the cyborg is showing signs of faulty behaviour, commonly associated with older cyborg models and calibration software. They intend to kill D6, but fail the execution process.

Later, D6 is found and restored by a galactic scrapper who specializes in refurbishing electronic devices and robotics, in order to make a living reselling them. When the scrapper realises the type of machinery he had found, he restores D6 and installs a communication module so that he can talk. Afterwhich, D6 goes on to take out the private corporation as his final life's mission, after realising not a single member of his kin remains alive, centuries after his transformation... all that remains in him, is the memories of them, and an empty isolated feeling remains.

# Purposes of Game Design

### Procedural Generation
- Procedural Generation: In my game, is the hopes for story telling in its own fashion. Take this scenario - a person wishes to share his own experience with another, and each experience is varyingly different from each other. The randomness of place, leads to new found and personal experiences which are interesting, it can lead to conversations about the experience upto a certain period of time, but also leaves room for a feeling of isolation and withdrawel. Edgar allan poe - And all I lov'd I lov'd alone. But here is why isolation and withdrawel is an aspect I wish to create in my procedurally generated universe. 

- On the feeling of isolation and withdrawel: Take our main character D6. Partially a cyborg, with only a certain percentage of his brain remaining, centuries since he has known his own name, his family and friends. Someone who really has fallen off the plate of life, conned into believing he was making a beautiful sacrafice to protect his people, to become part machine... and in the process... loosing himself for scientific advancement and all that he loved, just to become a heartless assassin without a flicker of consciousness left. Until he starts to remember. Which, in theory, would make any feeling of isolation far worse. Imagine coming to consciousness remembering your past and knowing only now the evil you had done. That seems quite screwed up and must therefore be unbelievable psychological suffering. And this is the feeling I will describe through my world building, street inspired music, grim story telling, terrible deeds and a final turning point to compensate for all that has happened. Whether redeemed or failed... this character must experience an isolating experience, of which I want the player to feel (In an artistic and compassionist way rather than mindlessly trying to cause suffering in people).

### Music and Art Style
- I have the feeling of street music. Street music I refer to as early origins of rap and the darker themes expressed through bass and drums, to create a sort of bad undertone to the fit the styles of these twisted lyrics that rappers would write.
- Considering the nature of our character in this game, and the things he will get up to - I feel that street music is the perfect fit for a bad boy atmosphere.

- On the Art style: When I was creating my game 'Darkness' for PC, I had theories about why a simple 2D platformer can be as interesting as any other form of game. No matter how the good the graphics are. The idea was as simple as this; How is a book a great and interesting form of entertainment? if not that the author's purpose and intention, is to articulate to the reader, a sense of place and relationship with that place, characters, and the journey. The sole thing I had identified was strictly 'Sense of place', because the mind is powerful and your experiences are very deeply made of mind.
- The second inspiration, was that there was a simple game that came out a while ago, where you simply play as shapes in a very basic 2D platformer with zero interesting art work. The creator of the game stated something along the lines that you can make a game interesting just by shifting the main focus of the game towards the relationships that the characters have in the game. As far as I can remember, each character had their own unique qualities and abilities that could aid other characters in different ways. Quite like how we are as humans, and how we all need each other to work as a team. This is relatable, and the as far as I can recall, the game was trying to explore those relatable relationships. I can't remember the name of the game, I never played it I just read about it but it had good feedback from its community of players. I am specificially interested in that effect of having an impact whilst my game is a very simple 2D game. I am not creating a game that explores relatable relationships, but instead using illusions of activity, to create a sense of place and understanding about characters, the story, and the activities you are engaged in, in order to make it more interesting and impactful.
